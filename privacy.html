Privacy Policy - TikTok Shop Automatico

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Overview

TikTok Shop Automatico ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

TikTok account data: When you authorize the App via TikTok OAuth, we receive a temporary access token and your TikTok Open ID. We do not collect your TikTok password.

Usage data: We may collect basic logs (timestamps, error messages) to operate and improve the service.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to publish videos to your TikTok account on your behalf, to identify trending skincare products for content generation, and to troubleshoot errors and improve the App.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except with TikTok (content sent via the TikTok Content Posting API) and service providers used to operate the App, bound by confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored only for the duration of the active session. We do not retain your TikTok credentials after the session ends.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok Settings > Security > Authorized Apps.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
